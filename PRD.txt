Nkiza Center for Research (NCR)
Advancing African and Global Knowledge through Accessible and Rigorous Publishing
The Nkiza Center for Research (NCR) is an independent academic publishing and research
dissemination platform dedicated to high-quality, affordable, and ethical scholarly publishing.
NCR places a strong emphasis on advancing knowledge relevant to Africa and the Global South
while remaining fully open to global scholarship and committed to international academic and
publishing standards.
Mission
To empower researchers, scholars, and practitioners by providing accessible, rigorous, and
ethically published research outputs that inform policy, drive innovation, and contribute to
sustainable development in Africa and across the globe.
Vision
To become a leading center for knowledge creation and dissemination that strengthens African
scholarship, fosters global academic collaboration, and supports evidence-based solutions to the
world’s most pressing social, scientific, and technological challenges.
Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
NCR actively supports the United Nations SDGs by promoting research and publications that:
• SDG 4 – Quality Education: Enhance access to knowledge through affordable and open
scholarly publications.
• SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure: Advance science, technology, and
innovation through rigorous research dissemination.
• SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities: Amplify the voices of researchers from Africa and the
Global South in international academic discourse.
• SDG 16 – Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions: Promote evidence-based policy,
governance, and ethical scholarship.
• SDG 17 – Partnerships for the Goals: Foster international collaboration and
interdisciplinary research networks for sustainable development.

C. SUBMISSIONS
Manuscripts submitted to the Nkiza International Journal of Computer Sciences, Engineering
and Data Sciences must be original, unpublished, and not under consideration by any other journal
at the time of submission. Researchers and practitioners from Africa and all other regions of the
world are encouraged to submit their work. Manuscripts must be prepared in accordance with the
journal’s author guidelines, ensuring clear problem definition, sound methodology or system
design, appropriate experimentation or validation, accurate citation, and adherence to ethical
research and publication standards.
All in-text citations and reference lists must strictly follow the referencing style adopted by the
journal (APA or another specified standard, depending on discipline). Submissions are made
electronically through the journal’s online submission system and are subject to plagiarism
screening prior to the peer-review process.
The journal respects authors’ privacy and intellectual property rights. Personal information
collected during the submission and review process is used solely for editorial and publishing
purposes and is not disclosed to third parties. Copyright of published articles remains with the
authors under an open-access licensing framework that allows users to read, download, share, and
cite the work with proper attribution. Authors grant the journal the right to publish, distribute,
archive, and monetize access to the article in accordance with the journal’s access and revenuesharing model, while retaining the right to reuse their work for academic, educational, and noncommercial purposes.

A. ABOUT NKIZA BOOK PUBLICATIONS
Nkiza eBook Publications is the digital academic and professional publishing arm of Nkiza,
established to support the creation, dissemination, and long-term preservation of high-quality
scholarly and applied eBooks. The imprint advances knowledge, education, professional practice,
and policy development through accessible, technology-driven digital publishing.
Unlike academic journals, Nkiza eBook Publications focuses exclusively on long-form digital
scholarship, including authored eBooks, edited eBook volumes, academic eTextbooks,
handbooks, professional guides, and policy-oriented digital publications. These works enable
sustained analysis, comprehensive treatment of subject areas, and enduring academic and
professional value.
Nkiza eBook Publications supports eBook projects across a broad range of disciplines, including
economics; medical sciences; natural sciences; social sciences, law and humanities; and
computer sciences, engineering and data sciences. While particular emphasis is placed on issues
relevant to Africa and other developing and emerging regions, the imprint welcomes high-quality
eBook proposals from authors worldwide, promoting inclusive knowledge exchange and global
academic engagement.

Nkiza eBook Publications considers proposals in the following categories:

## Executive Summary

The Nkiza Center for Research (NCR) is an independent academic publishing and research dissemination platform dedicated to high-quality, affordable, and ethical scholarly publishing with emphasis on African and Global South scholarship. The platform will manage five peer-reviewed journals and an eBook publishing arm, featuring a unique author-centered revenue-sharing model where authors receive 50% of article access fees and 70% of eBook royalties. ## Risk Management

### Technical Risks
- **Payment processing failures**: Implement multiple payment gateways and manual fallback options
- **Data loss**: Daily backups with geographic redundancy
- **Security breaches**: Regular security audits, penetration testing
- **System downtime**: Load balancing, redundant hosting

### Business Risks
- **Low submission rates**: Active marketing, competitive pricing, author incentives
- **Quality concerns**: Rigorous peer review, editorial training, plagiarism detection
- **Revenue shortfalls**: Diversified revenue streams (APCs, access fees, eBooks, subscriptions)
- **Indexing delays**: Early engagement with indexing services, metadata quality assurance

### Reputational Risks
- **Predatory publishing concerns**: Transparent editorial processes, qualified editorial boards, ethical guidelines
- **Author disputes**: Clear terms of service, dispute resolution procedures
- **Payment issues**: Transparent revenue sharing, timely payments, clear documentation [ppl-ai-file-upload.s3.amazonaws](https://ppl-ai-file-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/web/direct-files/attachments/71500252/a32a73a3-d972-4e55-abc9-de359f5a6834/paste.txt)
